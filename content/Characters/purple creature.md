    <h3>About</h3>
    <p>Zosimos is a seasoned alchemist who has spent decades wandering the world of Aetheria, studying the arcane properties of its creatures and materials. Weathered but sharp-eyed, he carries an old satchel full of vials, tomes, and trinkets collected over a lifetime of experimentation. He speaks with the unhurried confidence of someone who has seen everything twice — and survived it both times.</p>
    <p>When you first arrive in Aetheria, it is Zosimos who finds you, guides you through your first steps, and teaches you the fundamentals of monster alchemy. He is neither your mentor by appointment nor by obligation — he simply believes that knowledge passed on is knowledge multiplied.</p>
