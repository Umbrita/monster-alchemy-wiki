Wants to create a new world, because this one is rotten and sick.
Their “God” lives [[Aliksir]] on the moon of [[Behila]].
Wants the best individuals, they are the deserving ones.
Rejects old traditions, they are the cause of the sick.
Implements the coliseums as a series of tests to find good individuals.
People start fighting and training monsters, the ones that do can defeat the ones that don’t, so they spread faster with power and fear.
People start admiring them for their strength.
[[Sythel]] is one of the cities that resists the Order because they are a military state and can defend themselves. Also they are at war against the undead of [[Zenilia]] and the Order prefers to wait until they are weaker. They aren’t in conflict, there are some order followers in [[Sythel]], but that's why there is not a coliseum.
